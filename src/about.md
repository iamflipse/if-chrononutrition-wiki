# About this wiki

This wiki distills a single open-access review:

> Ma M, Yuan J, Xu F, Zhou Y, Li T, Zhou D and Zhang X (2026). "Chrononutritional regulation of metabolic homeostasis: intermittent fasting through gut microbiota, circadian rhythms, and autophagy." *Frontiers in Nutrition* 13:1964828. doi: [10.3389/fnut.2026.1964828](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnut.2026.1964828)

**Scope of the review:** systematic search (PubMed + Web of Science through 2026-07-31) of 76 eligible references covering randomized controlled trials and preclinical mechanistic studies of four IF patterns — time-restricted feeding (TRF), alternate-day fasting (ADF), 5:2, and 4:3 — and their effects on weight, glucose, lipids, and the underlying chronobiological mechanisms.

**Key caveat (from the paper):** most mechanistic evidence is preclinical (rodent models); human translation requires large-scale RCTs. IF also carries potential adverse effects (dizziness, hypoglycemia, hair-follicle issues), so individualized patterns and metabolic assessment matter.
