# Chrononutrition & Intermittent Fasting

**The one-line thesis:** Intermittent fasting improves metabolic homeostasis through **chronobiological mechanisms that operate beyond caloric restriction** — the gut microbiota–barrier–endotoxemia axis, circadian clock re-synchronization, SCFA-driven WAT browning, and SIRT3–AMPK–mTOR autophagy.
