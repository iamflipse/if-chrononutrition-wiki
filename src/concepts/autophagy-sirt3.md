# Autophagy via SIRT3–AMPK–mTOR

**Autophagy** is the cellular clearance/recycling system: autophagosomes engulf damaged organelles and misfolded proteins, fuse with lysosomes, and degrade contents for reuse. Starvation and oxidative stress are the canonical triggers.

**How IF activates it:**
1. Fasting lowers intracellular nutrients/ATP → **AMP/ATP ratio rises** → **AMPK** activated.
2. AMPK **phosphorylates ULK1** → autophagosome formation (with ATG13, ATG7), and simultaneously **inhibits mTORC1 via TSC1/TSC2**, relieving mTORC1's suppression of ULK1.
3. **SIRT3 sits upstream of AMPK** in this axis.

**Preclinical evidence (ADF and 16:8):**
- ADF in rats: ↑ autophagosome numbers in skeletal muscle; ↑ AMPKα, p-AMPK, ULK1; ↑ **LC3-II/LC3-I ratio**; ↓ **p62** → enhanced autophagic flux.
- ADF in HFD-obese mice: ↑ SIRT3, activated AMPK; **SIRT3 inhibition attenuated ADF's insulin-sensitizing effects** → confirms SIRT3 is upstream.
- ADF in HFD mice: ↓ mTOR phosphorylation in liver, ↑ LC3-II/LC3-I, ↓ p62, improved glucose tolerance and serum triglycerides.

Net effect: improved clearance of damaged cellular components and better glucose/lipid utilization → improved metabolic homeostasis (Figure 6 in the paper).

**Caveats:** all mechanistic evidence is from rodent models; whether IF specifically (vs. caloric restriction) drives autophagy in humans is unproven — no IF vs. CER comparison for autophagy outcomes exists.
