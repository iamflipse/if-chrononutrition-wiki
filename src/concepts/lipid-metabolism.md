# Lipid Metabolism

IF improves circulating lipid profiles (triglycerides, cholesterol fractions). The paper attributes these gains to three parallel mechanisms beyond the energy deficit:

1. **SCFA-mediated WAT browning** — fasting shifts the gut microbiota (↑ *Lactobacillus*, *Akkermansia*) and short-chain fatty acid (acetate/lactate) production; SCFAs upregulate MCT1 and thermogenic genes (**UCP1, PGC-1α, PRDM16**), converting white adipose toward beige. See [Adipose Browning & Thermogenesis](adipose-browning.md).
2. **Leptin-sensitized postprandial BAT thermogenesis** — refeeding after a fast surges leptin (~8-fold in 48-h-fasted rats), driving the hypothalamus–adrenal medulla–BAT axis; blunted in diet-induced obesity (leptin resistance).
3. **Suppressed intestinal lipid absorption** — IF remodels microbiota and suppresses the **PI3K/AKT → mTORC1 → SREBP1** pathway, reducing intestinal lipid uptake.
