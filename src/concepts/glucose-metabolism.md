# Glucose Metabolism

**Human evidence:** TRF, ADF, and 5:2 consistently reduce **fasting glucose, fasting insulin, and HbA1c** in overweight, obese, and diabetic populations. In type 2 diabetes, 5:2 fasting achieved HbA1c reductions **comparable to metformin or empagliflozin over 16 weeks**; early TRF (eating window ending before 15:00) improved insulin sensitivity in prediabetic men **without weight loss** (Sutton et al., Cell Metab 2018).

**The hard question — isocaloric head-to-head vs. CER:** Carter et al. (two independent trials) found **5:2 and CER produced nearly identical HbA1c reductions** at 12 weeks and 12 months in diabetes; in NAFLD patients, 16:8 TRF and CER gave comparable glycemic improvements at 6 and 12 months. So the glycemic benefit is **largely driven by the energy deficit**, with the chronobiological component not yet isolated in humans.
