# Weight Loss Effects

A network meta-analysis of **99 randomized trials** found most IF regimens achieve long-term (≥24 weeks) weight loss **comparable to continuous energy restriction (CER)**.

- Across TRF, ADF, 5:2, and 4:3, typical reductions range from **~1.5 kg (TRF) to 7.7 kg (4:3)**, depending on duration and adherence.
- **Eating-window length matters:** 6–8 h windows produce greater reductions than 10–12 h windows.
- **Adherence gap:** TRF compliance is 85–88% at 12 weeks, while long-term (2-year) adherence to 5:2 drops **below 30%**.
- Combined interventions (TRF + exercise or + energy restriction) enhance efficacy, but head-to-head vs. CER shows **no significant difference** in weight outcomes.

**The paper's key point:** IF's unique value for metabolic health may lie in chronobiological effects on glucose/lipid homeostasis, operating at least partly **independently of weight loss** — see [Metabolic Homeostasis](metabolic-homeostasis.md).
