# Adipose Browning & Thermogenesis

Two complementary thermogenic routes amplify energy expenditure beyond the fasting energy deficit:

## 1. SCFA-induced WAT browning (fasting-side)
- ADF raised the **Firmicutes/Bacteroidetes ratio** and upregulated the microbial pyruvate→acetate/lactate fermentation pathway.
- SCFAs (acetate, lactate) upregulate **MCT1**, which induces **WAT browning**: upregulation of **UCP1, PGC-1α, PRDM16**.
- Similar browning was observed with *Akkermansia muciniphila* colonization in germ-free mice.

## 2. Leptin-mediated postprandial BAT thermogenesis (refeeding-side)
- In obese mice, **16:8 IF** raised **postprandial VO₂, heat production, and BAT temperature**; abolished by BAT denervation or UCP1 deletion → intact BAT innervation + UCP1 required.
- Serum leptin was elevated; benefits were largely absent in leptin-deficient **ob/ob** mice → **enhanced leptin sensitivity** is the key driver.
- Rats: refeeding after a 48-h fast raised plasma leptin ~8-fold; the leptin surge drives the **hypothalamus–adrenal medulla–BAT axis** (catecholamines → β-adrenergic signaling in BAT, using fatty acids from adipose lipolysis).
- **Bolus feeding** (mimicking IF's meal pattern) increased body temperature and reduced weight gain vs. continuous isocaloric feeding; effect blocked by β-adrenergic antagonism.
- In diet-induced obese rats (chronically hyperleptinemic even while fasting), this feeding-induced thermogenic response is **blunted** (leptin resistance).

## 3. Suppressed intestinal lipid absorption
- The **PI3K/AKT → mTORC1 → SREBP1** pathway drives intestinal lipid uptake.
- IF remodels the microbiota (↑ *Lactobacillus*, ↑ *Akkermansia*) and suppresses this pathway, limiting lipid absorption in parallel with browning.

**Caveats:** WAT browning and PI3K/AKT evidence is rodent-based; the relative contribution of WAT browning vs. BAT thermogenesis to the total energy-expenditure increase has not been directly compared.
