# Intermittent Fasting Patterns

IF is classified by fasting duration and frequency into five patterns (TRF, ADF, 5:2, 4:3, and religious fasting). The four most-studied in obesity and glucose–lipid disorders:

- **Time-restricted feeding (TRF):** normal daily caloric intake confined to a 4–12 h eating window, fasting the rest of the day. Sub-types by window length: 12:12, 14:10, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4 — with **16:8 the most widely adopted** regimen.
- **Alternate-day fasting (ADF):** alternating fasting days (~500 kcal, or 20–25% of basal energy requirement) with ad libitum feeding days.
- **5:2 fasting:** two low-calorie days per week, ad libitum eating the other five days.
- **4:3 fasting:** three low-calorie days per week (~20% of basal metabolic rate), ad libitum the other four days.
- **Religious fasting:** structured abstinence in Christian, Islamic, and Buddhist traditions (less studied here).

The 16:8 TRF window is the default in most of the mechanistic studies cited in the paper.
